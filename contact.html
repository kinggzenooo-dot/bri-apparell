FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

We deliver nationwide across the Philippines. Orders within Metro Manila typically arrive in 2–3 business days. Provincial orders take 4–6 business days. We offer complimentary insured shipping on all orders over ₱2,500.
We accept returns on all unworn, unwashed garments with original tags attached within 30 days of receipt. Simply submit a return request through our concierge, and we will dispatch a courier to collect your parcel free of charge.
Our Essential Tees and French Terry Hoodies are intentionally cut with relaxed, boxy proportions. We recommend ordering your true standard size for the intended contemporary fit, or sizing down one full size if you prefer a classic tailored look.
We recommend washing cold (30°C) inside out with mild detergent. To preserve structural drape and avoid shrinkage, lay flat to dry in the shade. Do not tumble dry or bleach.
